About

Greetings! I’m currently a PhD student in the Data Science and Analytics (DSA) Thrust at the Hong Kong University of Science and Technology (Guangzhou) (HKUST-GZ), supervised by Asst. Prof. Jiaheng Wei (魏嘉珩). I received my master’s degree in Artificial Intelligence from Shenzhen University, where I was supervised by Assoc. Prof. Meng Xu (徐萌) and conducted research at the Hyperspectral Image Laboratory (HSI Lab). My research interests include computer vision, embodied AI, vision-language models, and spatial intelligence.

Research Interests

Three directions that shape how agents see and locate themselves in the world.

01

Embodied AI & Egocentric Vision

Perception and reasoning for agents that see and act in the physical world from a first-person view.

02

Spatial Intelligence & Multimodal Learning

Vision-language models and multimodal systems that understand space, geometry, and scene structure.

03

Cross-View Geo-localization

Matching and aligning imagery across viewpoints — ground, aerial, and satellite — for robust localization.
